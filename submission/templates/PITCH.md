# Three-sentence pitch

TritFlow takes balanced-ternary HDL through real standard cells, routing,
official DRC/LVS and extracted ngspice in one inspectable notebook.
Readers can connect gate equations to device behavior, see where loading and
wiring affect settling, and switch among arithmetic examples checked against
independent references.
The submission combines saved evidence with a reproducible open-source flow,
while making sampled coverage, static-current costs and model limits explicit.
