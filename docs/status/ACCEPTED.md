## What acceptance looks like

The corpus is evidence of the range organizers have accepted, not proof that a particular feature caused selection. The comparisons below distinguish a recurring useful practice from an actual rule.

1. **A concrete design question is more consistent than any particular endpoint.** CORDIC connects its angle/iteration choices to hardware (E06 c8–22); CDC turns conversion targets into a chosen design and simulated figures of merit (E11 c9–23,48–49); LDO-RL optimizes a schematic without reaching layout (E10 c76,85). TritFlow's single-wire three-voltage implementation and editable dot product already provide this substance. Do not add GDS or silicon requirements that the call does not contain.
2. **The most effective openings identify the artifact before the long explanation.** The SNN opening (M07 c3) states the software/hardware path and the concrete core/application in about 100 words. Retiming introduces its arithmetic library and use case, then shows an animation (M11 c2–5). FlashADC gives the repeated-block problem, three workflow steps, and a schematic before setup (M12 c3–7). Some accepted openings are long; this is an editorial opportunity, not a maximum-word rule.
3. **Methods are easiest to follow when their results occur beside them.** DLDO shows floorplanning through routing as successive pictures (E03 c19–30). DFFRAM connects behavioral RTL, mapped netlist, and generated layout (E09 c74–89). CDC follows parameter selection with layout, verification, and simulation. TritFlow's chapter 1 walkthrough, chapter 2 divider estimate, and chapters 3–5 dot4 sequence already follow this pattern; preserve them.
4. **Strong teaching exposes meaningful choices, not just a Run button.** CORDIC's iteration/pipeline controls (E06 c21–22), CDC's target/constraint controls (E11 c9–23), and GLayout's primitive/sizing/PDK controls (E12 c12,14,19) connect edits to circuit consequences. TritFlow already offers sizing, signal selectors, placement replay, waveform inspection, and HDL modification. More widgets are not a missing acceptance criterion.
5. **Useful results interpret a discrepancy or tradeoff.** LearnAFE relates the change from ideal to SPICE transfer functions to accuracy (M05 c80), then explains the learned-front-end power/capacitance tradeoff (c103). SJSystolicArray explains why the hardware image darkens under eight-bit arithmetic (M04 c28,31). Retiming translates slack into achievable clock period (M11 c29,32). TritFlow already explains divider-model error, routing growth, static current, wire loading, and the sign of STA error. Additional repeated result summaries are unnecessary.
6. **Length varies widely.** The 32 single-notebook projects span **26–182 cells, median 57**; their median code-cell share is approximately **44%**. The nine EEG component notebooks are a separate all-code set, not a 102-cell tutorial. Across all 41 notebooks: 2315 cells = 1087 code + 1227 Markdown + 1 raw. TritFlow's source edition is **64 cells, 31 code/33 Markdown**, and its package is 68 cells. Its overall length is ordinary for this set; selectively shorten the opening rather than removing technical chapters.
7. **Slow setup and computation are managed in several accepted ways.** GreenRio discloses a failed Colab route and uses local results (E04 c30–42); Open3DFlow requires Docker and a large image (M03 c35,43); FlashADC supplies local simulation results when Colab is too slow (M12 c49,57). CML preserves LUTs (R02 c16), LLMForge uses recorded data for hour/day-scale sweeps (R04 c16,27), and Wrøngm explains its roughly 10–12 minute run (R08 c4,19). TritFlow's recorded scaling/characterization with identity checks is well justified. Keep the live small flows and recorded larger campaigns.
8. **Pinning and failure handling are uneven, so preserve TritFlow's stronger existing implementation.** Examples pin OpenRAM v1.1.19 (E02 c10), gdsfactory 7.7.0 (E12 c7), or tool builds; others clone mutable branches. Saved outputs also contain interrupted exports, property mismatches, or incomplete optimizer branches. Acceptance does not certify that every cell ran successfully. TritFlow's immutable source/PDK references, source hashes, checked data, readable errors, and saved static figures are valuable already; the practical release task is to make all published copies refer to the same finished design.
