## Prose rules (Japanese narrative, binding)

である調, standard technical terms (配置配線, 論理合成, 抽出, ポストレイアウトシミュレーション); no plain-language
paraphrase, metaphors or poetic lines; do not restate what a log, table or figure already shows
(cell counts, pass/fail); never introduce a concept without defining it; HDL in its own cell; open
each chapter by saying what it explains and how it follows from the previous one; objective
register (results are "示す", no directions to the reader such as 〜してください, no first-person acts
for what a widget does); no legends for what the picture shows; no live checks that prove nothing
new. Numbers in prose must match the cell outputs. The integrator polishes every text change with
its own Japanese style checker before committing, so keep sentences plain and factual.
